16. EXPLAIN maxT VERSUS T_min EXPLICITLY
========================================

Create a manuscript-ready note explaining:

    component maxT and T_min answer different questions.

The component maxT test asks:

    whether a particular observed component is extreme after comparison
    with the largest null component.

The T_min test asks:

    whether the weakest of the observed standardized effects is unusually
    large relative to the null distribution of the weakest component.

Therefore:

    all three moderately aligned effects can yield a highly unusual T_min
    even when one marginal component misses maxT adjustment.
