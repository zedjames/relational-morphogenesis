# Reproducibility

## Public finite-result verification

Run:

    make verify

The verifier checks source authority, decisive numerical surfaces, final Methods-robustness summary objects, and the SHA-256 identity of the frozen manuscript/source/figures using the Python standard library.

## Source authority

Raw MOSTA data are not mirrored. Public accession and raw-project identifiers are recorded in `data/source_manifest.tsv`; source hashes and biological sample authority are recorded in `data/authority/`.

## Analysis reconstruction boundary

The private development environment contains the large carrier-building and graph-generation pipeline. This public companion releases finite manuscript-facing outputs and independent consistency checks. Frozen upstream commits and pre-outcome plans are recorded in `docs/METHODS_PROVENANCE.md`.

## Methods robustness

The final publication hardening explicitly separates the registered primary estimator from neighboring-model controls. Public finite outputs for pseudo-stage calibration, relation-support rebuilding, distance-weighted transport, equal metric weights, embryo-balanced discretization, deeper Curveball mixing, embryo-level support, physical radii, and potential-reference reconciliation are under `results/methods_robustness/`.

## Formal verification

Standalone publication-control theorem source is released under `formal/standalone/`. Dependency-backed checks are represented by formal verification receipts and exact completion commits. The public `make verify` target is deliberately finite and does not depend on the private theorem environment.
