## Biological question

How much apparent developmental-history dependence remains after the contemporaneous state is represented at higher molecular resolution, and how much additional predictive information is carried by reconstructed lineage?

## Public result boundary

The released surfaces support inspection of the complete matched-control witness analysis; fully inductive leave-one-embryo-out molecular prediction; state- and target-representation robustness; exact-key lineage exposure; regularized and joint-metric lineage models; the complete 76-member family-wise lineage calibration; the complete 125-bank ambiguity/support diagnostic family; finite-state qualification sensitivity; source/annotation authority; and publication-facing formal controls.

The principal positive result is that a training-owned molecular representation improves prediction over the coarse (S_0) comparator in each of the three held-out embryos under both signed-hash and training-fitted PCA target families. The complete lineage-model family does not establish an additional family-wise predictive contribution (`p = 0.553`). The complete discrete history diagnostic has zero multiplicity-corrected departures (`p = 0.31008`). No tested finite molecular partition is promoted as biological identity.
